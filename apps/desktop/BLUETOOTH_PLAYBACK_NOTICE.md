# Bluetooth Playback Notice

Meeting Intelligence Copilot records at 48 kHz. On macOS, Bluetooth headsets may switch into a
lower-quality hands-free profile when their microphone is active. Playback can then sound muffled
even when the saved recording is intact.

For accurate review, use computer speakers or wired headphones. If Bluetooth playback sounds poor,
disconnect the headset microphone or play the exported file after recording has stopped.

This limitation is caused by the operating system's Bluetooth audio profile selection. It does not
change the saved transcript, evidence identifiers, or live intelligence state.
